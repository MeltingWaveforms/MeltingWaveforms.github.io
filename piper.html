Melting Waveforms Artist

Piper Jorin

Pop • R&B • Synthpop • Indie Pop

Piper Jorin grew up on camera before she knew what that meant. Child actress, early start, LA from the beginning — she knows this city in a way most people only pretend to.

She left the spotlight quietly. No announcement, no drama. She just stopped showing up to the things that didn't feel real anymore and started paying attention to the ones that did. Now she works the front desk at a hotel where nobody knows her name from before, and she likes it that way.

Her music is warm, specific, and entirely her own. She's not trying to make it back. She chose this on purpose.

She left the spotlight and never looked back.
