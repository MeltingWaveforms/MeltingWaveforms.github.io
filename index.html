About Melting Waveforms

A label home for AI-generated music built to feel cinematic, modern, and alive.

Melting Waveforms is an evolving label concept where AI voices, neon visuals, and curated songcraft meet. 15 artists. Every one with their own sound, backstory, visual identity and emotional world.

Every sound starts with a human spark — an overheard phrase, a real feeling, a moment worth remembering. The AI generates raw material. Every lyric, every creative decision, every character is shaped by a human creative director. The result is something that sounds like real life feels.
